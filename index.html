    What is being played

    Every member of this jury is a Pollinations decision model, asked through POST /alpha/decisions with one typed choice question. A model answers with the option it picks and a probability for each option; the app averages those distributions and calls the highest one the verdict.

    The probabilities are the models' own, not truth. A confident jury can be confidently wrong, which is the point of betting against it. Names and measured status:

    You pay with your own Pollen through the device flow; the key stays in this tab and is sent only to gen.pollinations.ai. There is no backend and no database — your chips live in this page. See a bundled round without spending anything.
